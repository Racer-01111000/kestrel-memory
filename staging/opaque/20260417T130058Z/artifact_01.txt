Quantum error correction

Quantum error correction comprises a set of techniques used in quantum memory
and quantum computing to protect quantum information from errors arising from
decoherence and other sources of quantum noise. QEC schemes that employ
codewords stabilized by a set of commuting operators are known as stabilizer
codes, and the corresponding codewords are referred to as quantum
error-correcting codes. More at “Wikipedia”

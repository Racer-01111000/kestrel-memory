    What is quantum error correction?

      □ Quantum error correction is a set of techniques for protecting quantum
        information from errors caused by decoherence and noise by encoding
        logical qubits across multiple physical qubits. Unlike classical error
        correction, which copies bits, quantum error correction must work
        around the no-cloning theorem and avoid measuring quantum states
        directly.

# Emerging Methods

## Canonical restored items

- Multi-provider routing across Amazon / IBM / Google quantum backends, selected dynamically by HOST.
- Error mitigation techniques such as zero-noise extrapolation and probabilistic correction.
- Variational algorithms including VQE and QAOA.
- Quantum networking components such as entanglement distribution and quantum repeaters.

## Status

These items are treated as feasible or promising, but not fully standardized or mature.

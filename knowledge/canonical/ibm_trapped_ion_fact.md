
## IBM and trapped-ion relationship

- FACT: IBM's current main commercial quantum hardware platform is superconducting, not trapped-ion based. IBM public hardware materials describe superconducting processors and fixed-frequency qubit hardware in its production roadmap and systems pages.
- FACT: IBM has materially engaged with trapped-ion research and publications, including work on parallel entangling operations in fully connected trapped-ion systems and Shor encoding on trapped-ion quantum computers.
- FACT: The most accurate framing is that trapped ions are part of IBM's broader quantum research and comparative architecture understanding, but not the basis of IBM's current flagship commercial hardware platform.
